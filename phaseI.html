INCANTATION Phase I: Visual Essay

Create a photo essay or narrative consisting of a minimum of 10 original photographs. Later you will practice any necessary digital retouching with the photos that you take or you may entirely transforming using digital manipulation techniques. The photographic story/essay should reflect a nuanced reality that relates to the selected current issue. The ten unedited photos are due the second week of class along with an initial project proposal.
